AI for plant operations, built security-first

Industrial plants already hold the answers to most daily operational questions, split across an ERP, a technical knowledge base, and device telemetry. I originated, designed, and built a serverless AWS tool gateway on the Model Context Protocol that connects AI assistants to all three systems under strict operational security.

Operators, engineers, and managers ask in plain language about machine history, open maintenance tickets, device health, or standard procedures and get answers grounded in real records, with sources named in business terms. Around the gateway sit hybrid keyword-and-semantic search for documentation, daily monitoring summaries, and interfaces built for touch, gloves, and glare on the plant floor.

Security controls
IdentitySSO per person. Gateway-bound tokens.
PermissionsDeny by default. Grants by person and system.
WritesPrepare, then signed apply. Expiry and conflict check.
AuditChanges logged under the user’s identity.
SecretsManaged store. Kept out of prompts and documents.
LimitsResponse-size and time caps.
MonitoringHealth checks, alarms, and usage logs.

How I work

  1. Go to the problem

    I start from the failing unit, the site, or the log.

  2. Instrument it

    I make the device and pipeline show what is happening before changing anything.

  3. Ship the smallest fix that holds

    I deliver the hardware revision, firmware release, or cloud change and validate it on real units.

  4. Leave it operable

    I hand over runbooks, update paths, and diagnostics so your team owns the system afterwards.

About

I work as a forward-deployed engineer across the full stack of connected hardware: custom PCBs, microcontroller firmware, field connectivity, cloud infrastructure, edge AI, and operational agents. Over the last decade I have taken industrial IoT products from first prototypes through certification and production fleets across Latin America, the Caribbean, and the United States.

I serve as CTO at Sento Tech Labs, leading a team of 12 engineers across embedded hardware, connectivity, and cloud services. I hold a degree in Electronics Engineering from Universidad Simón Bolívar.

My master’s studies in electronics are at Universidad Simón Bolívar, with the thesis pending. The university awarded an honorific mention to my undergraduate thesis on underwater telemetry, and my team took first place in the IEEE Open Category at CCSBOTS 2013.
