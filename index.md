## AI for plant operations, built security-first

Industrial plants already hold the answers to most daily operational questions, split across an ERP, a technical knowledge base, and device telemetry. I originated, designed, and built a serverless AWS tool gateway on the Model Context Protocol that connects AI assistants to all three systems under strict operational security.

Operators, engineers, and managers ask in plain language about machine history, open maintenance tickets, device health, or standard procedures and get answers grounded in real records, with sources named in business terms. Around the gateway sit hybrid keyword-and-semantic search for documentation, daily monitoring summaries, and interfaces built for touch, gloves, and glare on the plant floor.

## How I work

1. Go to the problem. I start from the failing unit, the site, or the log.

2. Instrument it. I make the device and pipeline show what is happening before changing anything.

3. Ship the smallest fix that holds. I deliver the hardware revision, firmware release, or cloud change and validate it on real units.

4. Leave it operable. I hand over runbooks, update paths, and diagnostics so your team owns the system afterwards.
